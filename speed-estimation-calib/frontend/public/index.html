💡 We recommend Known-distance calibration: it gives the highest accuracy for CCTV and other fixed cameras.

How do you want to calibrate?

Draw TWO lines on the road. Click and drag for each straight line. Drag an end-point to rotate or resize it, or drag the line to move it.

Enter the real distances

Tip: Use road markings, lane boundaries, or other points whose real-world distance you know. Draw both lines in the same direction (P1 and P4 on the same side of the road).
